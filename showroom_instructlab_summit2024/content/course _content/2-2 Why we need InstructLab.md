

# Why we need InstructLab

Large Language Models (LLMs) have the ability to fuel a diverse array of practical applications, like having a virtual assistant that can chat with you, help you with coding, or even generate content for your next marketing email. 

However sometimes they need updating because:
    
- They need to be kept them fresh and up todate
- Models may need to be 'tweeked' pre-trained models to enhance elements of their knowledge
- There are limits to augmenting models with RAG
- Fine-tuning is highly skilled and complex
- Generic small models typically need to be educated in the area of expertise relevant to their business applicaiton

What ever the reason, updates are normally needed when models are applied to a business use.

## The Challenge with LLM's - and why we focus on SML's 

To adjust the parameters and train the open source model Llama 2-70B from Meta.ai, you need approximately 6000 GPUs and you would need to run the training for about 12 days, which ends up costing approximately **$2 million**.
