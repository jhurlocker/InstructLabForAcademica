# Advantages of Synthetic Data Generation


**Pros of using InstructLab to generate Synthetic are:** 
- it is information dense as you focus on a taxonomy divided into specific branches and leaves
- it fills gaps in training LLMs, 
- it can help boosting performance and reducing bias. 
- you control the quality of your training data (Context/Knowledge) 
- organisations or niche topics, may not have vast quantities of content so generating synthetic data can scale up and enhance for training purposes
- generating synthetic data with InstructLab is easy, fast, and has a lower cost than creating this manually.
- customisation for specific scenarios

**Potental Con:**
Since the data is being synthetically generated by LLMs, hallucinations are possible (However, InstructLab provides a Critic Model, and you can perform manual verification prior to training.)
